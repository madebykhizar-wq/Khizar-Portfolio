BIOXY

Bioxy is a wellness identity built around vitality and connection. Four radiating forms meet at a central point, bringing energy, cellular connection, and a subtle clinical cross into one distinctive mark inspired by BIO and OXY.

A deep ocean blue foundation is balanced with aqua mist, seafoam green, pebble gray, and white. Paired with Montserrat typography, the system carries a calm, contemporary identity across digital interfaces, packaging, and tactile print touchpoints.

Client Bioxy S.L.
Year 2026
Category Health & Wellness
Deliverables Brand Identity, Logo Design

01 — Mark Geometry & Rationale

The Bioxy symbol brings four radiating forms together around a central convergence point. The geometry connects organic cellular movement to oxygen, energy, and human vitality, while the interior negative space forms a subtle cross. This gives the brand an unmistakable mark grounded directly in the syllables BIO and OXY.

02 — Typography & Color Architecture

The symbol pairs with a custom BIOXY wordmark and a structured Montserrat type scale. Deep Ocean Blue provides clinical authority, while Aqua Mist and Seafoam Green introduce lightness and vitality. Pebble Gray, white, and Midnight Slate complete the palette, with tested reverse lockups for light and dark environments.

03 — Digital & Interface Applications

The identity extends into digital interfaces, mobile app iconography, and structured social layouts. Across varying resolutions, the primary mark and color proportions maintain immediate recognizability and visual weight.

04 — Packaging & Print Production

From product packaging label concepts to tactile foil-stamped business cards and stationery, the visual system translates seamlessly between screen and physical substrate.
