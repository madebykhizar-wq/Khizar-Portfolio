Technical Expertise

Frequently asked questions about packaging & identity.

Every packaging design is created directly on vendor-supplied or engineered dielines. Final files include strict bleed margins, safe zones, knockout/overprint settings, Pantone spot color callouts, and CMYK process values. Before production runs, I review digital or physical printer proofs to ensure substrate fidelity and ink accuracy.

A standalone logo is a single mark or wordmark. A brand identity system is a complete design framework: primary and secondary logo lockups, responsive scales, defined typography hierarchies, strict color proportions, negative space rules, iconography, and substrate standards that give your team consistent visual equity across all touchpoints.

Product line extension requires consistent Primary Display Panel (PDP) architecture. We establish fixed brand anchor zones (brandmark, category identifier, net weight) while varying flavor, scent, or variant indicators through controlled color coding and secondary typography. This ensures immediate shelf recognition while preventing consumer confusion.
