I'm Khizar — an independent brand identity and packaging designer based in Gujranwala, Pakistan. Over the past 6+ years, I have worked with consumer brands, food & FMCG businesses, and startups to turn rough ideas into confident, market-ready brand systems.

I help consumer brands, FMCG businesses, and startups build strategic visual systems — from positioning and typography to shelf-ready packaging dielines and production handoff. If your brand or product packaging needs clarity and shelf standout, that's where we start.
