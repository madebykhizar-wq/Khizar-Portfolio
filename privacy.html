Information you send

If you use the contact form, it asks for your name, email address and project details. The form is delivered through FormSubmit, a third-party form service, to the email address shown on this site. You can also contact me directly by email or WhatsApp.

I use your message to respond to your enquiry and, if we agree to work together, to discuss and manage that project. Do not send sensitive personal information through the form.

Website analytics

Optional Google Analytics is used to understand visits and general use of the website. It loads only if you accept analytics in the cookie settings. You can reject optional analytics or change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings button. If accepted, Google may use cookies or similar technologies; review Google's privacy information for details about its processing.

Hosting and other services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. FormSubmit, Google Analytics, and any services you open through an external link operate under their own privacy terms. Their handling of data is outside this site's control.

Storage and your choices

Your cookie choice is stored in your browser so the site can remember whether optional analytics are allowed. Enquiries received by email are kept for as long as needed to respond, manage related work and meet applicable obligations. You can ask me to access, correct or delete information from an enquiry by emailing madebykhizar@gmail.com. Some information may need to be retained where required by law.

You may have additional rights under the privacy laws that apply where you live. Contact me at the address above to make a request.

Changes and contact
